Description          "Prison Architect makes you hire every member of staff by hand, and a guard who dies or quits stays gone until you notice. Auto Hire adds a Staffing Terminal that keeps each kind of staff at the number you set.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and load your prison.\r\n[*]Build a Staffing Terminal ($1,000) anywhere. One terminal covers the whole prison, and if you build two, only the older one hires.\r\n[*]Click the terminal and press the Staff button until it shows the kind you want: guards, armed guards, dog handlers, snipers, workmen, cooks, janitors, gardeners or doctors.\r\n[*]Press -10, -1, +1 or +10 to set how many of that kind to keep, from 0 to 500.\r\n[*]Repeat for each kind. Hover over the terminal to see every number you have set.\r\n[/olist]\r\n\r\n[h2]How it works[/h2]\r\nEvery few seconds the terminal counts the living staff of one kind you have set a number for. When that kind is short, it hires up to five, who arrive at the terminal half a second apart, and then counts again. A body waiting for the hearse does not count. The screen is blue while the terminal is hiring and green once every post has somebody in it.\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]After a riot your staff numbers recover without you touching anything.\r\n[*]It never fires anybody. Set a number below what you have and it stops replacing people who leave until you are down to it.\r\n[*]Every hire pays the game's own hiring fee out of your balance and draws the usual wages.\r\n[*]Armed guards stop at the game's own limit of one per Guard Locker in an Armoury, and the terminal shows you that limit. Sell a locker and the number drops with it.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]What it cannot do[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]It hires whether or not the money is there, so a big order on a small balance puts you in the red.\r\n[*]A mod cannot read your research, so the terminal fills a number you set for staff you have not unlocked yet.\r\n[*]If you hire by hand while a batch is arriving, you can end up as many as five over.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nNone known. It adds one object and changes nothing the game already has. With Robot Staff on, the staff it hires arrive as robots."
